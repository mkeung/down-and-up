# Decisions made

A running log. Add new decisions here in the same change that makes them, and link the plan that carries the detail.

- **Reaction asset review:** prepare ten 01e animation studies mapped from 1 (carefree/no stress) to 10 (most stressed), using natural coat colors and changing expressions/posture. The user chose assets and an offline keypad gallery first. Keep these separate from app behavior; mobile continues to roam independently of values. See [visual direction](visual-direction.md).

- **Tapping the selected number again:** does nothing. A day's value can be changed but never cleared, and each day has at most one entry.
- **Labels:** none. The app is for mood but stays discreet, so there is no label, prompt, or mood wording anywhere.
- **Number layout:** phone keypad style, with 1–9 in a 3×3 grid and 10 alone, centered, on the bottom row.
- **Name:** keep "Down & Up".
- **Theme color picker:** Settings offers Neutral, Mint, Blue, Peach, Plum, and Yellow presets instead of requiring one fixed palette. The choice is local to each phone, autosaved in SQLite, and retained across restarts and ID changes. Light/dark follows the phone. Key surfaces remain neutral; data-status colors stay dedicated to storage. See [mobile app: theme colors](completed/mobile-app.md#theme-colors).
- **Visual direction:** use retro pixel, based on `Concepts/UI/03-retro-pixel.png`: crisp stepped panel edges, readable pixel numerals, neutral keycaps, and custom grid/paw tab art. Palette and control-art studies are in `Concepts/UI/RETRO-PIXEL.md`; color presets are available in Settings, while custom-control treatments remain exploratory. All ten keys keep one neutral color family, with selection shown by a bold neutral border and checkmark. Mobile UI implementation is tracked in [visual direction](visual-direction.md).
- **Official artwork:** preserve the Cerberus character version 01c in `Concepts/` as a reference study and use its full-body retro-pixel variant `OfficialAssets/01e-cerberus-retro-pixel-character.png`, matching icon 10. Use `OfficialAssets/10-cerberus-retro-pixel-icon.png` as the app-icon master. `scripts/icons.mjs` prepares `apps/mobile/assets/icon.png` from it. The icon is a chunky pixel portrait with three heads, three tails, and two small tabby front paws with the legs hidden. Move earlier icons 09b (opaque) and 09c (transparent) back to `Concepts/` as reference studies; 10 is the only official icon master. Exploratory UI studies and generation prompts stay in `Concepts/`.
- **App lock:** none.
- **In-app history:** a History screen with a list of values by day and charts, read from local data so it works offline.
- **Mobile tabs:** two tabs, **Rate** (keypad) and **Pawsitive** (note, plus recording once it ships), sharing one selected day. History and Settings stay as screens without a tab. Settings opens from the [data status icon](completed/data-status-icon.md). History has no visible entry point for now and opens through `down-up://history`.
- **Data status icon:** a red, yellow, or green icon in the top-right corner of Rate and Pawsitive shows whether entries are unsaved, saved on the phone, or saved to the cloud, and opens Settings. Each state also has its own shape. These status colors are the only colors outside the neutral palette and never relate to the value ([data status icon](completed/data-status-icon.md)).
- **Notes:** an optional text note (≤2,000 chars) for any day. An optional audio recording (≤5 min, one per day) comes later.
- **Voice recording deferred:** the first release ships without recording. The audio design (the recording UI, audio transfer, the audio routes, R2, and the cleanup cron jobs) is kept in [voice recording](voice-recording.md). The SQLite and Postgres audio columns and the audio field group in the sync protocol stay in place and empty, so turning recording on later needs no migration or protocol change.
- **Plan documents:** the plan lives in focused files under `docs/`, indexed by [`docs/README.md`](README.md). Fully completed plans move to `docs/completed/` and describe the app as built; active plans describe only what they change.
- **Past dates:** no limit on how far back.
- **No sign-in:** a device-generated **12-character** letters-and-numbers ID links the app to the web. The web login is just that ID. A hidden registration secret makes re-registering safe after a reinstall.
- **Sync consent:** a one-time notice on first launch. Sync is on by default, and "Keep on this phone only" is available.
- **Android Auto Backup:** disabled. The server is the backup.
- **Daily reminder:** optional. Turned on and timed on the web, delivered as local notifications scheduled by the phone in its local time. Skipped on days already logged. Neutral text ("Down & Up" / "Reminder").
- **Reminder permission deferral:** "Not now" defers the explanation until the next foreground; it does not count as an OS permission request. Denied requests expose phone-settings guidance even when the OS would allow another request ([reminders](completed/reminders.md)).
- **Delete my data:** either wipes the device too (fresh ID), or keeps local data and turns sync off.
- **Sync metadata:** each field has a dirty flag; acknowledgments clear only accepted groups unchanged since the push. Clamped timestamps also have replay receipts to make retries idempotent across changing server times.
- **Storage URLs:** audio object namespaces use a hash of the ID.
- **Very old dates:** dates before 1900 are local-only.
- **Mobile charts:** lightweight SVG charts, using the shared statistics helpers.
- **Identity transitions:** abort sync, journal the reset, cache remote recordings for Merge, and re-queue every field and file.
- **Replace ID:** generate server-side, copy audio before moving rows, invalidate sessions, and keep a short-lived retry receipt.
- **Hobby staging:** use two daily cleanup jobs twelve hours apart; both drain the deletion queue and the second also sweeps orphans. This preserves the 24-hour deletion target without a paid hourly cron schedule. Vercel cron is production-only, so preview cleanup is verified by manual invocation.
- **Phone-only cleanup:** delete unreferenced local recordings without waiting for sync. Discarding local data also removes cached CSV and ZIP exports.

- **Retro-pixel UI implementation:** use one shared stepped control system across every mobile palette, with bundled pixel type for numerals/chrome and regular type for notes and explanations. The keypad retains neutral keys and a checkmarked double frame; custom two-tab navigation uses pixel grid/paw glyphs. Rate and Pawsitive have transparent official Cerberus art that roams independently of entries. The web dashboard remains unchanged pending a separate design decision.

- **Unframed roaming character:** show the transparent cutout 01f of the approved full-body 01e artwork at the bottom of both main tabs, above navigation and outside the scrollable controls. No panel or white background. Slow native-driver motion is decorative and never tied to values. Pause on hidden tabs, background, Reduce Motion, or an unreadable accessibility preference, and hide during keyboard input. Preserve the opaque master and icon.

- **Roaming continuity:** mount one Cerberus instance in the navigation footer rather than one per screen. Rate/Pawsitive tab switches keep the same position and animation without resetting or restarting; hidden routes pause and retain the in-memory position. A new app launch starts at the center.

- **Everyday character playback:** enlarge the footer character from a 168pt still-image viewport to a 240pt animated sprite viewport. Use the approved walk loop during linear travel, randomly stop for sitting or tennis/yarn play, and sometimes extend seated rests to 14–30 seconds. Flip rightward walks and retain that facing throughout stationary animations. Bundle the four sheets and frame timings locally, keep playback independent of values, and preserve tab continuity and accessibility/background pauses. See [visual direction](visual-direction.md).

- **Keep 10 reachable:** pin the centered 10 key outside Rate’s scroller, keep all keys at least 64×64pt, and compact their vertical padding. The pet remains in separate space below 10, scaling down on shorter viewports so it never idles over the button. Settings and History omit duplicate bottom safe-area padding above the already-inset tab bar. See [mobile app](completed/mobile-app.md#number-buttons) and [visual direction](visual-direction.md).

- **Entry-screen layout and typography:** group all keypad rows below one shared Rate/Pawsitive header, with a clipped upper-row scroller for constrained heights and 10 always centered underneath. Match the note panel to the keypad’s scaled height. Use bundled Silkscreen for keypad numerals and dates; retain Pixelify Sans elsewhere. Open iOS date selection in a Cancel/Done overlay. Lower the character by removing transparent sheet margins, leaving 12pt above navigation. Explicit note Submit flushes the draft, dismisses the keyboard, and plays food-can once with the current facing; all behavior remains independent of numeric values and note contents. See [visual direction](visual-direction.md) and [mobile app](completed/mobile-app.md).

- **Connect keypad reactions:** at the user’s explicit request, replace the earlier ambient-only restriction with one-shot playback of the approved 1–10 reactions after changed values save locally. Preserve neutral copy/key styling, repeated-selected no-ops, current position/facing, background/tab pauses, and offline playback. Latest choice wins during rapid edits; failed saves, date/history reads, and sync trigger no reaction. Reduce Motion shows the final pose without playback. Ambient wandering and note Submit retain their existing behavior. See [visual direction](visual-direction.md) and [mobile app](completed/mobile-app.md#number-buttons).
