# Visual direction: retro pixel

**Status: mobile retro-pixel UI, theme picker, and app icon implemented; device accessibility checks remain.**

## Direction

Use retro pixel, based on [`Concepts/UI/03-retro-pixel.png`](../Concepts/UI/03-retro-pixel.png):

- crisp stepped panel edges,
- readable pixel numerals,
- neutral keycaps,
- custom grid and paw tab art for Rate and Pawsitive.

Additional palette and control-art alternatives are in [`Concepts/UI/RETRO-PIXEL.md`](../Concepts/UI/RETRO-PIXEL.md) (mint cartridge, midnight blue, peach tickets, plum stickers). Settings now offers Neutral, Mint, Blue, Peach, Plum, and Yellow color presets. All six presets share stepped controls and custom pixel tabs; the different control treatments in the boards remain reference studies.

## Rules the theme must keep

- All ten keys keep one neutral color family and identical art. Selection is shown by a bold neutral border and a green circle checkmark, so it doesn't rely on color. The same badge is used for every saved number and indicates saved content only.
- Palette color belongs to surfaces and chrome, never to the numeric scale. Saved-content badges retain their own green light/dark colors across palettes.
- The [data status icon](completed/data-status-icon.md) keeps its dedicated storage colors independently of the selected palette.
- Keys stay at least 64×64pt. Text scaling and contrast must be checked in the real app; the mockups are illustrative.
- No mood wording, emoji, or gradients. All numeric keys remain neutral. Cerberus roams gently at the bottom of Rate and Pawsitive, with no rewards or scoring. At the user’s explicit request, holding empty space in its reserved area starts a rope tug interaction. At the user’s explicit request, changing a value plays its corresponding 1–10 reaction once. Loading history, changing dates, and sync never trigger reactions.

## Done

- **Smaller mobile animations:** all seventeen mobile sprite sheets now use 256×256 frames in a 1024×512 PNG grid, exported with nearest-neighbor resizing and lossless PNG compression. The mobile sheets total 6.38 MiB instead of 28.16 MiB (77.3% smaller), and decoded RGBA pixels fall from 8 MiB to 2 MiB per sheet. Playback retains the eight frames, original timings, transparent margins, facing, ground alignment, and rope geometry. Full-size official sheets, frames, previews, and generated sources remain in `OfficialAssets/Animations/01e/`. Run `node scripts/cerberus-assets.mjs` to regenerate mobile exports and `--check` to verify them. Review the smaller sprites on iOS/Android devices before release.

- **Pawsitive confetti:** explicit note Submit adds a brief shower of pixel dog bones and yarn balls, alongside the existing food-can/backflip animation, once the keyboard closes. Draw the sprites locally with crisp vector steps and use native transforms/opacity for the 2.4-second fall. The overlay passes touches through, is hidden from screen readers, and works offline. Suppress it with Reduce Motion or an unreadable/loading preference; cancel it on route changes, backgrounding, keyboard reopening, or screen-size changes. Autosave, blur, numeric entries, history, and sync never trigger the shower. See [mobile app](completed/mobile-app.md#text-note-optional).

- **Sound settings:** mobile Settings adds a Mute toggle and continuous 0–100% volume slider below the theme picker. They control button and animal clips together, apply immediately, and persist locally in one `ui_sound` record across restarts and ID changes. Mute keeps the chosen volume, stops active clips, and cancels pending cues; zero volume is silent. Preferences load before playback, with a muted fallback for unreadable stored data. Screen readers adjust in 5% steps. Existing clip balance, silent/vibrate behavior, and mixing with other audio remain in place. See [mobile app](completed/mobile-app.md#sound-controls).

- **Audio concepts:** the user's requested satisfying button feedback and separate Pom/dog/cat voices have twelve listening candidates in [Concepts/Audio](../Concepts/Audio/README.md): six interface sounds and two alternatives per head. - **App sounds:** `src/sounds.ts` plays short clips through expo-audio (no microphone permission). Every button press, including all ten keypad keys, plays one shared click.
